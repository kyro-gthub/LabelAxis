# Moving, sizing and lining up objects

## Select

- Click an object to select it. `Shift`+click adds or removes objects from the selection.
- Drag across empty space to select everything the box touches.
- `Ctrl+A` selects everything on the label.
- Objects in a group are picked up together; `Ctrl`+click reaches one object inside a group.

## Move and resize

- Drag an object to move it. The arrow keys nudge it by one grid step; with `Shift`, ten steps.
- Drag a handle to resize. `Shift` keeps the proportions; `Ctrl` resizes from the centre.
- Exact positions and sizes are under `Properties > Position & Size`.

## Rotate and slant

- Drag the round knob above a selected object to rotate it; `Shift` snaps to 15° steps. `Alt`-dragging any handle rotates too.
- `Edit > Rotate 90° right / left` turns by a quarter; `Edit > Flip horizontal / vertical` mirrors.
- `Free transform` (`Ctrl+Shift+T`) turns the side handles into slant handles.

## Line things up

- `Align` on the toolbar lines up the selected objects by their left, centre, right, top, middle or bottom edge, spreads them out evenly, or makes them the same width or height.
- **Snapping:** while you drag, objects snap to the grid, to guides and to other objects. Turn each one on or off in the bar above the label (`Snap to: Grid, Guides, Objects`) or in the `Scale` menu.
- **Guides:** drag from a ruler onto the label to place a guide line. Drag it back to the ruler to remove it. `Same on every page` shares guides between pages; `Lock` stops them moving.
- **Grid:** shows a grid; `−` and `+` change its step.

## Front and back

Objects lower in the `Layers` list (on the right) are drawn behind those above them. Drag rows to reorder, or use `Ctrl+B` (bring to front), `Ctrl+Shift+B` (send to back), and `Ctrl+↑` / `Ctrl+↓` in the list.

## Group and lock

- `Ctrl+G` groups the selection so it moves and resizes as one; `Ctrl+Shift+G` ungroups.
- `Ctrl+L` locks an object so it cannot be moved by accident.

## Zoom

The mouse wheel zooms in and out where the pointer is; `Ctrl`+wheel scrolls up and down and `Shift`+wheel sideways. `Ctrl+1` fits the label in the window and `Ctrl+0` shows actual size. Hold `Space` (or the middle mouse button) and drag to move around.

## Undo

Everything can be undone with `Ctrl+Z` and redone with `Ctrl+Y`.
