# Your first label

LabelAxis designs labels and stickers and prints them on label sheets (A4 or Letter sheets of stickers, like Formtec or Avery), on thermal label printers, or saves them as PDF.

## Make a label in five steps

1. Choose `File > New Project…` (or `Ctrl+N`) and pick the label sheet you bought. Search by its product code, for example **3100**, or by size, for example **38x21**. Not on the list? See [[label-sheets|Label sheets and sizes]] to make your own.
2. Add what goes on the label from the toolbar: `Text`, `Image`, `Barcode`, `QR Code`, `Table`, `Shapes` or `Lines`.
3. Click an object to select it, drag it into place and drag its handles to resize it. Its settings are on the right, under `Properties`.
4. Press `Ctrl+P` to print. The picture on the right of the Print window shows the whole sheet exactly as it will come out.
5. Save with `Ctrl+S`. Projects are saved as **.labelproj** files.

> Tip: Before printing on an expensive sheet, print once on plain paper and hold it against a label sheet up to the light. If it is a little off, the [[calibration|printer calibration]] fixes it for good.

## Find your way around

- **Left:** `Pages` (the label designs in this project) and `Data` (a spreadsheet or database for printing many different labels).
- **Middle:** the label itself, with rulers, guides and a grid.
- **Right:** `Properties` (settings of the selected object), `Layers` (every object, front to back) and `Page` (the label sheet and the page on screen).
- **Bottom:** whether the project is saved, the label sheet, the record shown (when data is loaded), the zoom, and the printer with its calibration.
