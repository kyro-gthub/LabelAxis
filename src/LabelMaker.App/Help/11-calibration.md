# Printer calibration

Most printers put the page a millimetre or two away from where they are told, and some print a fraction too large or small. On labels that shows. Calibration measures your printer once and corrects every print after that.

## Calibrate a printer

1. Open `Print > Printer Calibration Wizard…` (or `Calibration wizard…` in the Print window).
2. Choose the printer and press `Print calibration target`. Use plain paper.
3. Measure the printed target with a ruler as the wizard shows, and type how far it is off: **X offset** (left/right) and **Y offset** (up/down) in millimetres, and the **scale** if lines come out longer or shorter than they should.
4. Press `Save calibration`.

The correction is kept for that printer and used every time you print to it. The Print window and status bar show whether the chosen printer is calibrated.

> Important: Calibrate with the same paper tray and settings you print labels with; a different tray can feed differently.

## Still off?

- Check the right **label sheet** is chosen; two sheets can look alike but differ by a millimetre. See [[label-sheets|Label sheets]].
- Make sure **Scale** in the Print window is 100% and the printer's own dialog is not set to "fit to page".
- See [[troubleshooting|Troubleshooting]].
