# Images

Click `Image` on the toolbar, or drag a picture file from Windows onto the label.

## Image Settings

- **Crop…** cuts away part of the picture; `Reset` shows all of it again.
- **Crop to shape** clips it to a circle or a rounded box (with its corner radius).
- **Fit** decides how the picture fills its box; **Allow stretching** ignores its proportions.
- **Opacity, brightness, contrast, grayscale** and **flip** change how it looks without changing your file.
- The resolution line warns when the picture is too small to print sharply (under about 200 DPI at its printed size).

## Outline border

Tick **Outline border** to draw a frame around the picture:

- **Thickness** in millimetres.
- **Colour**, from the swatches or the `…` colour picker.
- **Line style:** solid, dashed, dotted or dash-dot.
- **Lines:** single, or **double** (two thin lines with a gap, together as thick as the thickness).

The border sits just inside the image's box and follows a circle or rounded crop.

> Tip: For a logo that should look faint behind the text, lower its opacity and send it to the back (`Ctrl+Shift+B`).
