# Label sheets and sizes

A **label sheet** (also called a template) says how big each sticker is, how many fit across and down, and where the first one starts on the paper. LabelAxis comes with the Formtec range and others; you can add your own.

## Choose or change the sheet

Open `File > Label manager…` (or `Page > Change template…` on the right). Pick a category on the left, a sheet in the middle, and check the drawing on the right. `Use this sheet` switches the open project to it.

## Favourites

Press `☆ Add to favourites` above the sheet drawing. Starred sheets are listed first under **★ Favourites** in the Label manager, and first in the New Project list too. Press it again to remove the star.

## Make your own sticker size

In the Label manager press `＋ New custom size…` and fill in:

- **Sticker:** a name, the width and height in millimetres, and the shape: rectangle, rounded corners (with a corner radius) or round / oval.
- **Paper:** A4, Letter or another size, or your own paper size; portrait or landscape.
- **Layout:** the margins and the gaps between stickers, then either **Fit as many stickers as the paper holds** or an exact number across and down. **Centre the stickers on the paper** puts the block in the middle.

The drawing on the right updates as you type. `Save` keeps it under **My templates**; `Save and use` also switches the project to it. Your own sizes can be changed with `Edit…` or removed with `Delete` (projects that use one keep their own copy).

> Tip: Measure a real sheet with a ruler: the distance from the paper's top edge to the first sticker, from its left edge, and the gaps between stickers. Those numbers go straight into the form.

## Change the label size of a project

`Page > Label size…` (or `File > Label size…`) changes the size of the label you are designing:

- Type a new **width and height**, or pick a size from the list of label sheets underneath (favourites first; search by code or size, such as **38x21**).
- **Scale the objects** stretches everything on the label to the new size.
- **Apply to every page** changes all the pages in the project.
- After picking a sheet, **Also print on the … sheet** switches the project to that sheet as well, so the printout matches.

## Thermal label rolls

Labels on a roll for a thermal label printer start with `File > New thermal label…`. See [[thermal|Thermal label printers]].
