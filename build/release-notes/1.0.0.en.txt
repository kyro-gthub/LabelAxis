LabelAxis 1.0.0

New
• LabelAxis in Arabic and Central Kurdish (Sorani): menus, windows, messages and the help guide, laid out right to left. Choose it in File › Settings › Language. Technical names such as PDF, QR Code and Wi-Fi stay in English.
• Automatic updates: LabelAxis looks for a newer version once a day and installs it for you when you choose Update now.
• Wi-Fi printers can now be calibrated like any other printer.
• Arabic text in exported PDF files can be selected, copied and searched.
• Your own fonts: add a .ttf or .otf file with "Add font…" next to the font list (or in Settings › Fonts), without installing it in Windows. A project that uses it keeps a copy, so it looks the same on another PC.
• 66 ready-made label designs on the first tab of File › New Project: price tags, product and barcode labels, food, shipping, stock, school, pharmacy, badges and QR labels, each on its own sheet or roll.
• A new look: see-through glass panels over soft colour glows, with eight themes (Frost, Aurora, Midnight, Graphite, Ember, Ocean, Sakura, Sage) in Settings or from the theme button at the top of the window.
• Curves in custom shapes: any dot can be a smooth curve instead of a sharp corner.
• New Project also has Custom size… for a label of any size.
• Apply page format to other pages (Edit menu, or right-click a page): every object on the other pages takes the position, size, rotation and look of its match on this page — font, colours, barcode type and size, borders — while each page keeps its own words, barcode values and pictures. It can also add the objects a page is missing.
• Pages are numbered 1, 2, 3… in the page list, the first page too.
• A tidier status bar that no longer overlaps on smaller screens.
• Gradients in the colour window: a shape's fill and a text box's background can blend two colours at any angle. Pick from 16 ready-made gradients or the ones you used recently.
• Find (Ctrl+F) also searches page names: pick a result to open that page, and Replace renames it.
• The barcode image generator saves PNG pictures (150 to 1200 dpi) as well as SVG and PDF.
• Your own designs: File › Save page as my design keeps a page with its sheet, fonts, pictures and data connection. It is listed first in File › New Project under My designs.
• GS1 barcode builder (Data menu): GS1-128, GS1 DataMatrix and GS1 QR codes for food, medicines, medical devices (UDI) and shipping, with check digits, dates and every GS1 rule checked as you type. GS1 DataMatrix and GS1 QR codes now hold true GS1 data.
• Print requests from other programs (Print menu): a till, an online shop or a spreadsheet prints labels by dropping a .json or .csv file into a watch folder, or by sending it to a web link on this PC. It can print or return a PDF.
• Your own theme: Settings › Make your own theme (or the theme button) picks your colours, background and glow, shown live; text colours are chosen so they always read.
• Check a printed barcode (Data menu): a webcam, a photo or a scan grades the barcode A to F the way a barcode verifier does, says whether it matches the label, and what to change.

Smaller download
• The main installer is now much smaller. On a PC without the Microsoft .NET 10 Desktop Runtime it downloads it once from Microsoft; an offline installer with everything inside is available too.

Also
• A new label starts with its sample text in the interface language.
• Screen readers read buttons and menus in the interface language.
• Maximising the window fits the screen exactly, also on PCs where it covered the taskbar or stopped short of an edge.
• Choosing a colour after "No colour" gives a visible colour; it used to stay see-through.
• Gradients in exported PDF files run the same way as on screen and can fade to clear.
• A long GS1 barcode on a small label is made to fit the label, and the text under any barcode shrinks to the width of its bars.
• About LabelAxis is only in the Help menu now.
